How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer) and tap Download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager, just this once.
  3. Tap Install, then open Leader and start your journey.

Your saved games stay on your phone. To update, download the newest APK and install it over the old one.

Check the file (optional)

Each release also has a .sha256 file. Compare it with the downloaded APK's checksum on the releases page, where older versions are kept too.
