Privacy Policy: Leader: The Political Journey

Version 2. Developer: Blacktree Soft.

Summary

The game collects no personal data. Nothing leaves your phone. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. The game requests no internet access.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, saves, character, settings and the small cartoon-avatar choices stay on this device only. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Photo for your cartoon (optional)

If you choose a photo to make your cartoon, you pick it with the Android photo picker (the game never gets access to your gallery). It is analysed on the phone only, using Google ML Kit's on-device face detection, to choose a skin tone, hair, beard, nose and mouth. The photo is not saved, uploaded or shared.

What we never collect

  • Name, e-mail, phone number or contacts
  • Location, microphone or files
  • Advertising ID or any identifier shared with other apps

Permissions

Only Vibrate (for short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings).

Fiction and resemblance to the real world

LEADER is a work of fiction and a political simulation made for entertainment. It is inspired by the general atmosphere of real-world elections, but it is not a news, polling, forecasting or advocacy product. Everything in it is invented, even where it feels close to real life.

  • Party names: All political parties, alliances and fronts are fictional. Any similarity to the name of a real party, past or present, is coincidental.
  • Symbols, flags and logos: All party symbols, flags, colours and logos are original artwork made for the game. They are not copies of, and are not endorsed by, any real party, organisation or official election symbol.
  • Persons and characters: All leaders, candidates, rivals, allies, advisers, voters and heirs are made-up characters. Names, faces, cartoon avatars, backgrounds and personalities do not depict any real living or dead person. Any resemblance to a real person is purely coincidental.
  • Maps, regions and constituencies: The maps, states, districts, constituencies, cities and towns are fictional or loosely stylised. Their shapes, boundaries, populations, voter mix and results are invented for gameplay and do not describe any real place or any real electoral roll.
  • Locations and venues: Rallies, offices, meeting places and other settings are imaginary and are not tied to real addresses or real events.
  • Questions, quizzes and choices: Every question, dilemma, option and consequence is written for the game. The outcomes are made up and are not opinions, advice or predictions about real parties, policies or elections.
  • Actions and events: Campaigns, speeches, deals, scandals, alliances, court cases, results and other events happen only inside the game. They are not reports of, or claims about, anything that happened in real life.
  • Numbers and results: Votes, seats, funds, ratings and opinion polls are generated by the game and are not real data.

The game does not promote, attack or take sides with any real political party, leader, community, religion, region or ideology. If you think something in the game resembles a real person or party too closely, please write to us at the address below and we will review it.

Age

The game is intended for people aged 18 and over because of its election and political themes. It is not directed at children.

Your rights and contact

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For any question write to blacktreesoft@protonmail.com.

Changes

If the game ever collects anything, this policy and its version number will change and you will be asked again before anything is sent.
