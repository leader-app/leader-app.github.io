From Nobody to the Nation's Leader

A single-player political career game for Android. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and playable fully offline.

The theme

You begin as an 18-year-old college student with no power. Every problem that stops you on the road is a test of character, and how you answer is the game. Your choices build a reputation that decides which parties approach you, which voters trust you, and whether one day you can lead a state or the nation.

Responses define you

Every problem has three answers with clear trade-offs.

Losing is a chapter

A lost election changes your path. It does not end your story.

No guaranteed success

Stats raise the odds. Chance and rivals keep it real.

The whole state matters

Every election shows the full picture, not just your seat.

A whole life in politics

Play one leader's career from college campus to ward, Assembly and Parliament, and perhaps the highest office. When your story ends, an heir can carry your family's name forward.

LEADER is a work of fiction. All parties, people, symbols and events are invented and do not represent any real party, person or official symbol. It is for players aged 18 and over.
