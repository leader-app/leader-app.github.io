Game features

Everything you can do in one political lifetime.

🧑

Create your leader

Choose a name, hometown, college and background. Optionally make a cartoon of yourself from a photo, analysed on your phone only.

🗣

Answer the people

Groups stop you with questions. Pick one answer and watch the crowd react. Choices shift your trust, popularity, influence and integrity.

🎨

Illustrated journey

Walk through illustrated college, village, city and capital scenes, one year at a time.

💼

Your office

Money matters, donations, party offers and alliance talks. Funds, risk and reputation move with every decision.

🗳

Elections

Choose a seat, plan a campaign, ride the campaign trail, then watch votes counted live, round by round, for your seat and the whole state.

🤝

Parties and alliances

Join a party or found your own, grow it state by state, switch sides, form governments and weather collapses.

📈

Climb the ladder

Student leader, corporator, MLA, minister, Chief Minister, MP and Prime Minister. Invitations come with real stakes.

👪

Family and legacy

Name an heir, browse your family tree and election history, and play on through the generations.

🎶

Music and reactions

Background music for each stage of the journey, celebration and defeat stings, vibration cues, and a mute button always at hand.

🔒

Your data stays yours

No account, no ads, no analytics and no internet needed. Everything stays on your phone.

💾

Save slots

Keep several lives at once and pick up any of them later.

🎨

Themes and settings

Light and dark themes, sound and vibration switches, volume control, and a tour you can replay.
